REGULATORY NOTES

Source: the European Commission's CBAM guidance documents published on 14
August 2026 (explanatory, not legally binding). Legal texts take precedence.

1. The reporting period is the calendar year in which the goods were
   produced.
2. For iron and steel products, only direct emissions count. Electricity
   emissions are not counted.
3. Complex goods add the embedded emissions of their precursors. Steel rod is
   a precursor of screws and nuts (CN 7318).
4. The precursor mass counted is the mass consumed, including the part that
   becomes scrap. Scrap carries zero embedded emissions.
5. Actual precursor data from another installation may be used only if it
   comes from a verification report issued by an accredited verifier that covers
   the period when the precursor was produced. Otherwise the default value
   applies.
6. If a precursor comes from several suppliers or years, the default is a
   weighted average by mass. A more specific attribution needs sufficient
   evidence.
7. Only stationary combustion counts. Fuel for vehicles and mobile machinery
   does not.
8. Plating, cutting, welding and finishing are excluded process steps.
9. Records must be kept for at least 6 years. The monitoring plan and the
   emissions report must be in English.
10. Specific embedded emissions (SEE) of a complex good = own attributed
    direct emissions per tonne + the sum over precursors of (mi x SEE of the
    precursor). mi = tonnes of precursor consumed divided by tonnes of product
    made.
11. Round total embedded emissions to whole tonnes and specific values to at
    most 5 decimals.
12. The importer needs, for iron or steel products: the mass percentages of
    Mn, Cr, Ni and other alloy elements, tonnes of scrap used per tonne of
    product, the share of pre-consumer scrap, and the main reducing agent of the
    precursor if known.
13. Per precursor supplier, the operator needs: producing installation and
    period, specific direct and indirect embedded emissions, production route and
    extra parameters, the verification report covering the production period, the
    specific embedded free allocation, and carbon price information if any.

CONTEXT FOR THE PITCH ONLY (not used by any calculation in the app; confirmed
via law-firm and trade-press sources quoting the regulations, Oct 2026):

14. CBAM factor for 2026 is 97.5 percent, so 2.5 percent of the obligation is
    payable for 2026. The app does not calculate the obligation.
15. The penalty for unsurrendered certificates is EUR 100 per t CO2e and
    falls on the EU authorised declarant (the importer), not on the operator.
16. The first annual CBAM declaration is due on 30 September 2027.
17. Importers below 50 tonnes of CBAM goods per year are exempt.
