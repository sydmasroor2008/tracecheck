TEST ORACLE: the Commission's worked example

Source: Commission Guidance No. 5d, Example 3 (production of screws and nuts
from purchased steel rods), 14 August 2026. These are illustrative example
values. They are not default values and not a real factory.

Carbon steel screws and nuts
- precursor SEE: 1.567 t CO2 per t
- product made: 17,000 t per year; steel consumed: 20,000 t; scrap: 3,000 t
- mi = 20,000 / 17,000, shown by the Commission as 1.176
- natural gas: 3.5 GJ per t of product; emission factor 56.1 t CO2 per TJ
- own direct emissions per t = 3.5 / 1000 x 56.1 = 0.196 (rounded to 3
  decimals)
- precursor part = 1.567 x 1.176 = 1.843 (rounded to 3 decimals)
- total SEE = 2.039 t CO2 per t

High-alloy (stainless) screws and nuts
- precursor SEE: 1.783; product 8,200 t; consumed 10,000 t; scrap 1,800 t; mi
  = 1.220
- same gas data, own direct emissions 0.196
- precursor part = 1.783 x 1.220 = 2.175
- total SEE = 2.371 t CO2 per t

Tests must show: with intermediate values rounded to 3 decimals the results
are exactly 2.039 and 2.371. With no rounding they are within 0.002 of those
values.
